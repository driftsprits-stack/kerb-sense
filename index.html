How to play

  • You only see where you look. Move your cursor (or drag on a phone) to aim your vision. Traffic outside the cone is still there — you just can't see it.
  • Green means cars are braking, not that the road is clear. Look both ways before you step out, every time.
  • Cross between the dashed lines. Crossing there on green pushes the wall back.
  • The warning triangle means don't cross yet. A speeding car is about to take the whole lane and it will not stop for you.
  • Your message can wait. A new notification just shows as a small badge — it never interrupts a crossing. Finish crossing first, then tap it once you're safely back on the pavement to answer it. Left unread too long, it costs you anyway.
  • Keep moving. The wall matches your pace — it only gains ground when you hesitate or cross unsafely.
